# Lecture 3: Typography and choosing fonts

![Google Fonts](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/065-practice-webdesign/raw/branch/main/docs/images/03-google-fonts.png)

*Screenshot: Google Fonts ([source](https://fonts.google.com/))*

## Key points

- Font choice changes the message and mood.
- Serif fonts (small strokes on the letters) feel traditional and authoritative; sans-serif fonts feel modern and approachable.
- Legibility and readability decide how fast information is processed.
- Use at most two fonts and combine contrast, for example a serif heading with a sans-serif body. Avoid fonts that look unprofessional.

## What we gained

- We can choose a serif/sans-serif pair on purpose.
- We know to limit a design to two fonts.
