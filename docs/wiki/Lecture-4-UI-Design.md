## Key points

- Hierarchy guides the eye: larger, bolder and higher-contrast elements are seen first.
- An accent color and large call-to-action buttons direct attention.
- Keep lines at 40 to 60 characters, align text consistently with few alignment points, and use white space, which raises perceived value.
- Design for the target audience.

## What we gained

- We can build a visual hierarchy with size, color and space.
- We can check a layout for line length, alignment and white space.
