# Lecture 2: Understanding color theory

![Adobe Color wheel](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/065-practice-webdesign/raw/branch/main/docs/images/02-adobe-color.png)

*Screenshot: Adobe Color wheel ([source](https://color.adobe.com/))*

## Key points

- Colors carry meaning: red is energy and love, yellow is joy and attention, green is freshness and safety, blue is trust and stability, purple is royalty.
- A palette must fit the brand message; inconsistent colors weaken a logo.
- Palettes: analogous (neighbors on the wheel, harmony), complementary (opposites, contrast), triadic and square. Adobe Color helps to explore them.

## What we gained

- We can pick a palette from a brand message instead of by taste.
- We can build analogous and complementary palettes in Adobe Color.
