## Key points

- Keep it simple and organized; clutter overwhelms users.
- Be consistent, especially in navigation.
- Readers scan in F and Z patterns; place content to match.
- Design for all platforms (responsive) and test with real users.
- Avoid dark patterns: tricks that push users into actions against their interest.

## What we gained

- We can review a page for simplicity, consistency and reading pattern.
- We can recognize dark patterns and avoid them.
